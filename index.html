Key Features

Everything You Need to Reclaim Control

Built by parents, for parents. Every feature designed to reduce your mental load while improving outcomes for your kids.

Tiered Access Levels

Three automatic modes: Full Free Use (good grades + chores done), Limited (some slip), and Homework/Research Only (needs improvement). No manual switching required.

Edsby Integration

Connects directly to your school's Edsby platform. Grades are pulled automatically — no manual entry, no trust issues, no arguments about what the teacher actually said.

Automated Chore Verification

Parents define chores with photo or check-in requirements. Kids complete them through the app. System verifies and unlocks access automatically.

Undefeatable Enforcement

Cloud gateway routes all traffic through a WireGuard VPN tunnel. Works with any router. Kids can't disable it, uninstall it, or bypass it with a VPN of their own.

Precommitment Tools

Cooling-off periods prevent emotional overrides. Locked strict mode requires a partner's approval to disable. Accountability partners keep everyone honest.

Scales to Multiple Families

Built as true SaaS from day one. Manage one child or ten. Perfect for blended families, co-parenting arrangements, or even small schools and tutoring centers.

How It Works

Set It Up Once. Let It Work Forever.

No complex network configuration. No daily management. Just connect, configure, and watch the system handle the rest.

1

Connect Gateway

Plug in the EduGuard cloud gateway (or use our virtual appliance). It creates a WireGuard tunnel that captures all household traffic.

2

Link Edsby

Enter your Edsby credentials (or school code). EduGuard securely syncs grade data on a schedule you control — hourly, daily, or on-demand.

3

Set Rules

Define your family's access tiers. "A average = full access." "C or below = homework only." "Chores incomplete = limited." You decide.

4

Live Your Life

The system runs 24/7. Kids see their status in real time. You get weekly reports. No more arguments, no more monitoring, no more guilt.

What Kids See

A simple, non-shaming dashboard on their devices. Current access level, what's needed to unlock more, and a clear view of their progress. Transparency builds trust.
